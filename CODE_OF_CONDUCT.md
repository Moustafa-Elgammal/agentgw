# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
be kind, assume good faith, keep feedback about the work and not the person, and do not
harass anyone. Unacceptable behavior can be reported privately to the maintainer through
GitHub. The maintainer may remove content or ban participants who break these rules.
